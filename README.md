Marketing-Campaign-Performance-Analysis

This project analyzes marketing campaign performance across locations, campaign types, target audiences, impressions, clicks, conversions, and engagement.

🔍 Overview

The Marketing Campaign Performance Dashboard provides an interactive analysis of campaign effectiveness and audience behavior.

The dashboard focuses on:

* Campaign performance
* Audience targeting
* Clicks
* Impressions
* Conversions
* Engagement
* Geographic performance

The analysis identifies which campaigns, audiences, and locations generate the strongest marketing results.

🎯 Objectives

* Identify the best-performing campaigns.
* Analyze audience engagement.
* Compare campaign conversions.
* Identify high-performing locations.
* Understand audience click behavior.
* Determine the most engaging marketing channels.

❓ Business Questions

* Which campaign generates the highest conversions?
* Which campaign has the largest target audience?
* Which audience segment records the most clicks?
* Which location generates the most impressions?
* Which marketing channel has the highest engagement?
* Where should marketing resources be concentrated?

📚 Dataset Description

Dataset: Marketing campaign performance data

* Campaign Type

🧹 Data Cleaning and Preparation

* Removed duplicate records.
* Standardized campaign categories.
* Cleaned location and audience fields.
* Checked numerical metrics.
* Organized campaign and audience categories.
* Prepared data for dashboard visualization.

📈 Key Trends Discovered

* New York recorded the highest company count.
* Influencer campaigns had the highest target audience.
* Men aged 18–24 recorded the highest click share at 20.10%.
* Chicago recorded the highest impressions.
* Influencer campaigns generated the highest conversions.
* Email recorded the highest engagement.

📊 Dashboard Development

The dashboard includes:

* Campaign performance analysis
* Target audience analysis
* Click distribution
* Location-based impressions
* Conversion analysis
* Channel engagement

💡 Recommendations

* Increase investment in high-converting Influencer campaigns.
* Develop targeted campaigns for high-engagement audiences.
* Strengthen Email marketing strategies.
* Focus marketing efforts on high-performing locations.
* Optimize lower-performing campaigns.

Conclusion

The dashboard demonstrates how campaign type, audience targeting, location, and marketing channels influence campaign performance. Influencer campaigns stood out for conversions, while Email showed strong engagement.
